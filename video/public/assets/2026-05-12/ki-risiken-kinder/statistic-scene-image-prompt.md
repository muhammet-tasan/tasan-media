**Purpose:** Atmospheric documentary B-roll background for statistic scene

---

## Image Generation Prompt

```
SCENE: Documentary-style B-roll for statistic about teenage AI chatbot usage

VISUAL CONCEPT:
A moody, intimate evening scene capturing a teenager's digital life environment. 
The image should evoke the quiet, private moment of a young person using technology 
in a domestic space — but shot in a cinematic, observational documentary style.

SPECIFIC IMAGERY:
- Teenager (partially visible or hands only, in foreground or background)
- Smartphone or laptop screen glowing (cool blue/white light)
- Domestic evening setting: bedroom, living room, or desk area
- Warm ambient light from lamps or window (sunset/dusk)
- Cool light from the screen creating light/shadow contrast
- Real, lived-in space (not sterile, not staged)
- Natural shadows and depth of field

ATMOSPHERE:
- Calm, introspective, not anxious or alarming
- Documentary realism, not stylized or overly dramatic
- Evening/nighttime mood (day ending, digital world prominent)
- Intimate scale (small moment, personal experience)
- Trustworthy, observational tone

PHOTOGRAPHIC STYLE:
- Shallow depth of field (blurred background for focus)
- Cinematic lighting with shadows
- Warm/cool color contrast (tungsten + screen light)
- 16:9 aspect ratio, high resolution (2K+ for documentary detail)
- Documentary photography aesthetic (not stock photo, not AI-generated looking)

COMPOSITION NOTES:
- Subject positioned off-center (not centered in frame)
- Negative space on right/top for text overlay
- Foreground blur or atmospheric depth
- Interest in hands, screen, or silhouette

COLOR PALETTE:
- Warm: #D4B896 to #C49060 (tungsten lamp tones)
- Cool: #1E3A5F to #2A5A8C (screen/window light)
- Dark: #0D0F15 (shadows, room depth)
- Overall: muted, not saturated; warm/cool contrast

WHAT TO AVOID:
- Anxious or scary imagery
- Stereotypical "addiction" framing
- Generic stock photography
- Overly bright or cheerful mood
- People looking directly at camera
- Staged or artificial composition
- Corporate or educational presentation aesthetics

FINAL TREATMENT:
This raw image will be heavily treated before use:
- Blurred/softened significantly
- Darkened (brightness ~50%)
- Overlaid with gradients and vignettes
- Grain texture added
- So the final result is atmospheric, not intrusive
```
