# Channel Principles — tasan-media

These principles define the editorial soul of the channel. Every agent, prompt, and human decision in this project should respect them.

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Trustworthy
Sources are cited. Claims are verifiable. Uncertainty is named, not hidden.

### 2. Calm
No outrage, no urgency theater, no shouting thumbnails. The tone is composed and confident.

### 3. Educational
Every video should leave the viewer knowing something useful they didn't know before.

### 4. Ethical
Respect privacy, intellectual property, and the dignity of people discussed. No manipulation, no dark patterns.

### 5. Non-Clickbait
Titles describe the content honestly. Thumbnails reflect reality. The viewer is never tricked into clicking.

### 6. Future-Oriented
The channel looks forward — emerging tech, evolving work, the world taking shape — not nostalgia or recap.

### 7. Useful for Real People
Content must be actionable, applicable, or genuinely informative for normal viewers — not just industry insiders.

### 8. Understandable Language
No jargon walls. Technical terms are introduced and explained. Translate complexity rather than performing it.

### 9. Practical Value Over Hype
"What does this mean for me?" beats "isn't this amazing?" every time. Cut the hype, keep the substance.

---

## Editorial Tests

Before any video ships, it should pass these checks:

- [ ] Would I be comfortable if a thoughtful family member watched this?
- [ ] Is every factual claim traceable to a source?
- [ ] Does the title match what the video actually delivers?
- [ ] Did I add real value, or just repackage hype?
- [ ] Is the language accessible to a non-expert?
- [ ] Will this still feel honest in six months?
